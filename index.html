epigenetics

Guessing how old a mammal is from its DNA

I built an epigenetic clock that predicts age from DNA methylation, across more than 12,000 samples from loads of mammal species and tissues (37,000 CpG sites each). The plot twist: a plain Elastic Net beat LightGBM and a neural network, with MAE 0.076 on relative age and Pearson r = 0.937.

Python, scikit-learn, LightGBM, Optuna, pandas, NumPy

the details
  • Way more features than samples, lots of them correlated, and big differences between tissues and species
  • Compared Elastic Net, LightGBM and a neural network; tuned with Optuna; 5-fold cross-validation
  • Sparse regularisation handled the correlated features, and it stayed stable even for species with few samples
  • Lesson: on high-dimensional biology data, simple and regularised can win, and you can actually interpret it

brains · MSc thesis

How the wiring changes across the cortex

Your cortex runs from areas that handle the senses to areas that do the higher-order thinking. Using diffusion MRI from 77 people, I looked at how connections change along that gradient. Connection length followed it more closely than local connection strength. Got a 9.0 / 10 for it, too.

Python, diffusion MRI, network analysis, variogram modelling

the details
  • Lausanne500 atlas (~450 regions), connectomes from tractography, geodesic distances across the cortical surface
  • Fitted a variogram per region: the range (how far its connections reach) and the sill (how strong its local connectivity is)
  • Range vs. hierarchy: left r = 0.26 (pperm = 0.00014), right r = 0.18 (pperm = 0.00723)
  • Range vs. distance from sensory cortex: left r = 0.41, right r = 0.44; variogram fits R² ≈ 0.98

ancient DNA · BSc thesis

What was in a cave hyena’s gut?

I rebuilt the gut microbiome of an extinct cave hyena from 40,000 to 50,000-year-old DNA and compared it with living carnivores. Some bacteria, like Clostridium and Lachnoclostridium, show up across species, which fits the idea that hosts and their microbes evolve together.

Python, R, ancient DNA, metagenomics, phylogenetics

the details
  • 8,685 microbial taxa across 19 mammal species, from shotgun metagenomics and 16S rRNA
  • Host phylogeny from mitochondrial DNA, plus probabilistic evolutionary models to find conserved lineages
  • UniFrac and PCoA to compare communities, permutation tests for significance
  • Main phyla: Firmicutes (42%), Proteobacteria (18%), Actinobacteria (16%), and composition tracked how the hosts are related
