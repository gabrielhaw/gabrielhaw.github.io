Interview questions

Questions I’ve been asked while applying for data jobs. If you’re job hunting too, maybe they help.

  1. Business caseIf a company’s in-store sales are stable but its e-commerce sales are dropping, what are the first things you would look at?

  2. Business caseA textile company sells materials such as silk and wool to customers like Zara and H&M, and wants to keep or strengthen those relationships. How would you decide which ones to prioritise?

  3. SQLWrite a query that groups sales data by customer and calculates the total amount each customer spent in August.

  4. MetricsWhat do accuracy, precision and recall mean? When would you use each one?

  5. Time seriesHow would you evaluate a time-series forecasting model? Which metrics would you use, such as mean absolute error?

  6. ModellingWhen would you use a simple model rather than a more complex one? Which hyperparameters would you consider tuning?
