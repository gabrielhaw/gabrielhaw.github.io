Building bytli

bytli is a social food journal. The idea is simple: where your friends actually eat, not where an algorithm thinks you should.

  • Log the places you eat
  • Tag the friends you went with
  • Plan food trips
  • See on a map where your friends have eaten

Full disclosure: it’s being vibe coded. My friend Fraser and I needed something to do in our spare time, so we started building it. It runs on React, Vite and MapLibre, with Supabase underneath.
